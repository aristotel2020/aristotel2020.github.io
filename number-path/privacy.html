Off By One privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Off By One is a single-player number puzzle game made by the developer of Off By One ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short, we don't collect any information about you. The phone app can show optional ads from Google AdMob, and Google handles the information those ads need.

The game itself

  • Your score, your chosen board size and any active points boost are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases.
  • We don't run any analytics or tracking of our own, and we have no servers that receive your data.
  • The game doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes your saved progress.

Ads in the phone app

The Android version of the game has an optional button that plays a rewarded video ad. Watching one doubles the points you earn for the next 24 hours. An ad only plays when you tap that button. Google AdMob provides these ads. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • information about your device and the app, such as the model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads, plus diagnostic data such as crash and performance information.

This information goes straight to Google, not to us. Google explains how it uses it in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app uses Google's consent message to ask for your consent before ads are shown. You can change your answer at any time with the Ad privacy choices link under the puzzle. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

Off By One is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you have a concern about a child's use of the game, please contact us.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't receive any. The only other company involved is Google AdMob, which provides the ads in the phone app, as described above.

Security and how long data is kept

Information sent to Google is encrypted while it travels. Data stored on your device stays there until you delete the app or its data. Google keeps ad data according to its own policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy in Off By One, contact the developer using the email address on the Off By One page in Google Play.
