Shape Pop privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Shape Pop is a single-player arcade game made by the developer of Shape Pop ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: we don't collect any information about you. The phone app can show optional ads from Google AdMob, and Google handles the information those ads need.

The game itself

  • Your high score is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases.
  • We don't run any analytics or tracking of our own, and we have no servers that receive your data.
  • The game doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. Its sounds are generated on your device.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes your high score.

Ads in the phone app

When a round ends, the Android and iPhone versions of the game can offer a rewarded video ad that gives you 15 more seconds. An ad only plays when you tap the button that offers one. Google AdMob provides these ads. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • information about your device and the app, such as the model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads, plus diagnostic data such as crash and performance information.

This information goes straight to Google, not to us. Google explains how it uses it in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app uses Google's consent message to ask for your consent before ads are shown. You can change your answer at any time with the Ad privacy choices button on the game's start screen. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iPhone you can control tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

Shape Pop is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you have a concern about a child's use of the game, please contact us.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't receive any. The only other company involved is Google AdMob, which provides the ads in the phone app, as described above.

Security and how long data is kept

Information sent to Google is encrypted while it travels. Data stored on your device stays there until you delete the app or its data. Google keeps ad data according to its own policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy in Shape Pop, contact the developer of Shape Pop using the email address on the Shape Pop page in Google Play.
