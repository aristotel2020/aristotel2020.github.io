Gestures & shortcuts

  • Pen draws. Hold the pen's side button to erase.
  • Two fingers: pan, pinch to zoom, twist to rotate (snaps straight near 0°).
  • One finger drag: pan. Hold one finger: pick a color.
  • Two-finger tap: undo. Three-finger tap: redo.
  • Select tool: draw a lasso, rectangle or ellipse. Painting, erasing, Fill and Clear only affect the selected area. Tap the select button again for shape and add / subtract.
  • The mirror button flips the view (not the picture). The lock button freezes pan, zoom and rotation.
  • Your palm is ignored while the pen is near the screen.
  • Pencil: tap the brush button again and pick Pencil. It has paper grain. Turn on Pressure → opacity to press lightly for a grainy line and harder to fill it in; with it off, the line is even and Density sets how grainy it is.
  • Brush settings: tap the active brush or eraser button again. A test stroke at the top shows what the brush will paint.
  • Filters (three-circles button): Gaussian blur and Noise, with a live preview; Apply or Cancel. With a selection, only the selected part of the layer changes. Noise only adds grain where something is painted; "New pattern" rolls a different grain.
  • Transform (box-with-corners button, or T): moves, scales and rotates the selected part of the layer, or the whole layer with no selection. Drag inside the box to move, corners to scale, edge dots to stretch, the round knob to rotate (snaps at 45°). Two fingers still pan and zoom the view. "Selection outline" moves just the selection, not the pixels.
  • Rectangle and ellipse selections line up with the screen, even when the canvas is rotated.
  • Clip ↓ (layers panel): the layer only shows where the layer under it has paint, like a mask; you can still paint anywhere on it and turn Clip off again to see everything. Several clipped layers in a row all clip to the first unclipped layer below them. Hiding that layer or lowering its opacity applies to its clipped layers too. Merge ↓ on a clipped layer bakes it into the layer below for good (Undo still brings it back).
  • Tapping the canvas closes any open panel without painting.
  • While you draw, bars and sliders the pen comes near fade away so you can paint under them; they come back when you lift the pen (switch it off in the menu: Hide UI near the pen).
  • Keyboard: B brush, P pencil, E eraser, I eyedropper, S select, M mirror, K lock view, Ctrl+D deselect, Ctrl+Alt+G clip layer, [ ] size, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y, 0 fit, L layers, mouse wheel zoom, Space+drag or middle drag to pan.
  • Your painting is saved automatically on this device.
