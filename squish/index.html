About Squish

  1. Load a picture: drop it, paste it, or pick a file.
  2. Paint the parts you want squishy. Cheeks work great.
  3. Grab and pull, then let go. Tap for a little boing, or use two fingers at once.

Hit Record GIF to save a clip. Pick a transparent or green-screen background if you want to use it on a stream or in another app.

Everything happens in your browser. Your picture never leaves your device.
