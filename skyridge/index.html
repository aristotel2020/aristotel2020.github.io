Flying tips

  • This is a real flight model. The plane doesn't go where the nose points. Lift, drag, thrust and weight decide. The green circle on the HUD is your flight path marker: where you're actually going.
  • Take off: full throttle, keep the centerline with L2/R2 (the engine pulls slightly left), and at about 55 kt ease back on the stick. Climb at 70–80 kt.
  • Turning: roll into a bank, then gently pull to tighten the turn. A touch of rudder in the same direction keeps the slip ball centered.
  • Stall: below ~55 kt clean (~48 kt with full flaps), or if you pull too hard, the wing stops lifting. The horn sounds: push forward and add power.
  • Trim removes the stick pressure for a given speed. Use the D-pad, or click L3 to auto-trim.
  • Landing: line up early, flaps down (R1 twice), fly ~65–70 kt. The PAPI lights left of the runway show two white and two red when you're on the 3° glide path. Just above the runway, cut power and flare gently. Under 300 fpm is a smooth landing. Brake with ✕.
  • The world goes on forever. There are no walls. Five airfields are marked on the minimap.
