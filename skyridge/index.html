Two flight modes

  • Natural is the real flight model. The plane doesn't go where the nose points: lift, drag, thrust and weight decide. The green circle is your flight path marker, where you're actually going.
  • Arcade is point and fly. Click the view to capture the mouse, then move it (or the left stick, or the arrow keys): the white aim circle moves and the plane banks, turns and climbs to follow it. Stalls are softened, the engine is stronger and the landing gear is tougher. Throttle and brakes still work the same. Esc frees the mouse and pauses.

Missions, points & rockets

  • Missions earn stars (beat the par times) and points. Stars unlock more missions. Spend points in the Hangar on new aircraft and paint.
  • Rockets: hold the crosshair (or the aim circle) on a target for a moment and it locks (red box). Rockets fired while locked steer themselves onto it.
  • Rearm & repair: stop on any airfield for two seconds.
  • Beacons: 20 light beams are hidden across the world in free flight, worth points each.

Flying tips

  • Take off: full throttle, keep the centerline with rudder (the engine pulls slightly left), and at rotation speed (55 kt in the SR-1) ease back on the stick.
  • Stall: fly too slow or pull too hard and the wing stops lifting. The horn sounds: push forward and add power.
  • Landing: line up early, flaps down, slow down. The PAPI lights left of the runway show two white and two red on the 3° glide path. Just above the runway, cut power and flare gently. Under 300 fpm is a smooth landing.
  • The world goes on forever. Five airfields are marked on the minimap.
